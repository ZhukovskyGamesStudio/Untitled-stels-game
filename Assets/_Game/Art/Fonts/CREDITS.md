# Fonts

The interface's fonts (the paper form and sticky notes look, `UiPalette` / `AssetBuilder.UI`). All under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](OFL-PT.txt): free to use, embed and ship in a game; the font files may not be sold on their own.

| File | Font | Role | Source | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PTSans-Regular.ttf | PT Sans Regular (ParaType) | body text: the printed form | github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ptsans | [OFL](OFL-PT.txt) |
| PTSans-Bold.ttf | PT Sans Bold (ParaType) | headings, buttons | github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ptsans | [OFL](OFL-PT.txt) |
| PTSansNarrow-Bold.ttf | PT Sans Narrow Bold (ParaType) | form captions in capitals, stamps | github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ptsansnarrow | [OFL](OFL-PT.txt) |
| PTMono-Regular.ttf | PT Mono (ParaType) | typed-in values: numbers, timers, key names (has the arrows) | github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ptmono | [OFL](OFL-PT.txt) |
| Caveat.ttf | Caveat (variable, default weight) | handwriting on sticky notes | github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/caveat | [OFL](OFL-Caveat.txt) |
